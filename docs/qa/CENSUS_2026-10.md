### 3a. Israeli basketball cups are unregistered (#230)

`סופרקאפ` (33 corpus rows) and `גביע ווינר` (16 rows) are not competitions in the
registry. Without them, a bare `מכבי`/`הפועל` text has no sport evidence and no
competition:

- `הצגת בכורה: מכבי זכתה בסופרקאפ מול הפועל`: rated push, hidden.
- `דרבי על תואר: מכבי תל אביב והפועל תל אביב ייפגשו בסופרקאפ`: stored `sport=unknown`, `injury`.

## Owner review (2026-10-12)

Before any calibration (#237), the owner blind-re-rated the **58 rows where the current
engine and his census rating differed by two notches or more**. He saw neither his
earlier rating nor the engine's decision.

- **28 of 58 ratings changed, and 26 of those 28 moved toward the engine's decision.**
  Most moves are one notch (hide<->low, feed<->low). A few are large: the Cyprus move went
  hide -> push, the Super Cup win push -> feed, and a Dietz statement push -> high.
- **What it means:** on borderline articles, owner ratings repeat only about half the
  time. Part of what this report counted as engine error was rating noise. After the
  review, Guy's shown precision is 92-95% (fit/held-out halves, current engine).
- **For every future census:** expect a noise band of about one notch. Small groups (n<5)
  cannot carry a calibration, and a big disagreement should be re-rated before it is
  acted on.

The calibration analysis that used this review is `backend/scripts/census_calibration_fit.py`
(read-only; seeded fit/held-out split; `--review` merges re-rated rows, `--candidate` scores a
proposed profile change).

`census2_ratings.json` now carries the reviewed values (28 lines changed). The raw review
is in `census2_review_ratings.json`, and the pre-review ratings are in git history. The
scored numbers in sections 1-5 above predate the review.

## Method

- `rate_census.py build --since 2026-08-01` (seed 20260916), with census #1's ratings copied in so its 91 rows were not re-asked. One stratum, weight 1.0, seeded shuffle.
- `feed_ground_truth.py score --ratings census2_ratings.json --sample census2_sample.json --live`.
- The causes in §2–§4 come from joining each disagreement to its stored facts in the corpus DB (read-only).
- **Not a gate yet.** The N05 282-item stratified gate stays in force. Turning this census into the regression gate is a separate decision.
